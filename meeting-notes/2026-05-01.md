### Introductions & Personal Goals
The goal of this working group is twofold: ease the user onboarding experience and decentralizing the matrix ecosystem by avoiding the `matrix.org` instance being used by default for newcomers.

We had a few new faces in the meeting, so we took some time to introduce ourselves and share our personal goals for the working group.

- Clement and Nicolas had a call with Hannah Aubrey from Mastodon GmbH, to exchange about running a list of federated servers. It was only half an hour, but very insightful.
    - It's not enough to just maintain a list, you also need to promote it and help the servers on the list to be successful and build a comunity around it.

    - The Website of matrix.org could need a lot of rework to be more welcoming and less "techy" for newcomers, but also to be more clear about the different options available for users. We should reach out to the foundation about this.
