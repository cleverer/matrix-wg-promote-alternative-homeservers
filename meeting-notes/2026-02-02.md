### Introductions & Personal Goals
The goal of this working group is twofold: ease the user onboarding experience and decentralizing the matrix ecosystem by avoiding the `matrix.org` instance being used by default for newcomers. 

### Rough timeline of what to do when
This "first" month would be an experiment on how we can work together and what we can archive, our first goal would be:
* 02.03.2026 -> Have a first draft of the policy (that already went through a few reviews and PR)

### How do we want to work together (Github Repo ok?)

Our work and documents should be easily accessible, collaborative and not "vendor/tool" locked in anywhere. 
Currently it will be on Github but that can be subject to change in the future.
