### Have a look at MSC4485

https://github.com/matrix-org/matrix-spec-proposals/pull/4485

Question is: are we already prepared to go into the spec phase?

To get the the spec process going, you need a concrete implementation.

"It takes however much time it takes to finalized an MSC"

Cinny and [Komai](https://github.com/etkecc/komai) is really eager to implement this, as does [continuwuity](https://continuwuity.org/), so we already have some support from people that would implement a working prototype.

Nicolas feels: we don't yet have the "Business Requirements" to already discuss concreted implementations.

Potential Benefit of following through with MSC4485 early: having a clear Opt-In process + in Real time adjustment, no danger of having to do an opt-out process.

Proposed way forward:
1. Finish Policy
2. Come up with architecture proposal until next month
3. Give MSC more attention and start implementing after we have a shared understanding of the target architecture: MSC4485, but also other potential new MSCs
